## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Impact KPIs
- **Carbon Sequestration Rate**: Tons CO₂/hectare/year
- **Biodiversity Index**: Species count and health score
- **Soil Health Score**: Composite score (0-100)
- **Water Quality Index**: Multi-parameter assessment
- **Land Restoration Progress**: Hectares restored/year

### Financial KPIs
- **Revenue per Hectare**: Annual revenue/managed area
- **Carbon Credit Revenue**: Monthly carbon credit sales
- **Practice ROI**: Return on regenerative practice investment
- **Cost Efficiency**: Cost reduction through optimization
- **Grant Success Rate**: Percentage of successful grant applications

### Operational KPIs
- **Data Accuracy**: Verification success rate (>95%)
- **User Engagement**: Daily active users and session duration
- **System Reliability**: Uptime percentage (>99.9%)
- **Response Time**: Average system response time (<2s)
- **Mobile Usage**: Percentage of mobile vs desktop usage
