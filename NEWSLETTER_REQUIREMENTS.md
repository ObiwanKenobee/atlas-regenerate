# Newsletter System: Functional & Non-Functional Requirements

## Functional Requirements

### 1. Newsletter Subscription (FR-NS)
**FR-NS-001**: Email subscription with validation
**FR-NS-002**: Double opt-in confirmation process
**FR-NS-003**: Subscription preferences (frequency, topics)
**FR-NS-004**: Unsubscribe functionality with one-click
**FR-NS-005**: Subscription status tracking

### 2. Content Management (FR-CM)
**FR-CM-001**: Newsletter template creation and editing
**FR-CM-002**: Rich text editor with media support
**FR-CM-003**: Draft saving and version control
**FR-CM-004**: Content scheduling and automation
**FR-CM-005**: Template library management

### 3. Subscriber Management (FR-SM)
**FR-SM-001**: Subscriber list management and segmentation
**FR-SM-002**: Import/export subscriber data
**FR-SM-003**: Subscriber analytics and engagement tracking
**FR-SM-004**: Automated list cleaning (bounces, unsubscribes)
**FR-SM-005**: GDPR compliance tools

### 4. Campaign Management (FR-CAM)
**FR-CAM-001**: Email campaign creation and sending
**FR-CAM-002**: A/B testing for subject lines and content
**FR-CAM-003**: Automated drip campaigns
**FR-CAM-004**: Campaign performance analytics
**FR-CAM-005**: Send time optimization

### 5. Analytics & Reporting (FR-AR)
**FR-AR-001**: Open rate and click-through rate tracking
**FR-AR-002**: Subscriber growth and churn analytics
**FR-AR-003**: Revenue attribution from newsletter
**FR-AR-004**: Engagement heatmaps and insights
**FR-AR-005**: Custom reporting dashboards

## Non-Functional Requirements

### 1. Performance (NFR-P)
**NFR-P-001**: Newsletter load time < 2 seconds
**NFR-P-002**: Support 100K+ subscribers
**NFR-P-003**: Email delivery rate > 99%
**NFR-P-004**: Admin panel response time < 1 second

### 2. Security (NFR-S)
**NFR-S-001**: Email validation and spam protection
**NFR-S-002**: GDPR and CAN-SPAM compliance
**NFR-S-003**: Secure admin authentication
**NFR-S-004**: Data encryption at rest and in transit

### 3. Reliability (NFR-R)
**NFR-R-001**: 99.9% uptime availability
**NFR-R-002**: Automated backup and recovery
**NFR-R-003**: Failover mechanisms for email delivery
**NFR-R-004**: Error handling and logging

### 4. Usability (NFR-U)
**NFR-U-001**: Mobile-responsive design
**NFR-U-002**: Intuitive admin interface
**NFR-U-003**: Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1)
**NFR-U-004**: Multi-language support

## Success Metrics
- **Subscription Rate**: 15%+ conversion from visitors
- **Open Rate**: 25%+ average across campaigns
- **Click Rate**: 3%+ average engagement
- **Unsubscribe Rate**: <2% monthly churn
- **Delivery Rate**: >98% successful delivery
